How to do it
  • Stand. One hand on your belly above the navel, one on your upper chest.
  • Inhale through the mouth for 2 counts. Belly hand moves out. Chest hand does not move.
  • Exhale on sss — teeth nearly closed, tongue tip behind the bottom teeth.
  • Stop the clock the moment it wobbles, flutters, or fades. That's the number, not how long you can make noise.
  • 6 reps, 15 seconds rest between.
